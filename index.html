Arc Player

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Applies to com.aeswox.arcplayer

Arc Player is a video player for files you already own: videos on your device and videos in your own Telegram cloud. This policy applies to the Google Play build and the GitHub-distributed APK.

The short version

The developer operates no server. Arc Player contains no ads, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or tracking. Your library, watch progress, favorites, and preferences stay on your device. Network requests are limited to the services described below.

What stays on your device

  • Telegram sign-in session. Session keys are stored in Arc Player’s private storage and used only to communicate with Telegram. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Watch progress and preferences. Resume positions, favorites, shelf layout, and settings are stored locally in app preferences.
  • Offline copies. Videos saved offline are ordinary files in Movies/Arc Player/. They can be removed from Arc Player’s Downloads page or any file manager.
  • Search queries. Arc Player does not keep a search-history log. Queries are sent to TMDB only to retrieve the requested metadata and artwork.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Telegram. Signing in sends your phone number and one-time code to Telegram’s servers. Streaming or downloading cloud videos transfers those files between Telegram and your device. This is Telegram’s service under Telegram’s privacy policy. Telegram sign-in is optional; local playback works without it.
  • TMDB. To show posters, titles, and descriptions, Arc Player sends movie or show names and search terms to TMDB’s metadata API. No account identifier, device identifier, or location is included. See TMDB’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub — APK build only. The GitHub-distributed build checks GitHub’s public release API for updates and downloads an APK if you choose to update. The Google Play build contains no updater; Play delivers updates.
  • Your own TV on the local network. When you pair an Android TV, the phone can serve your local videos to that TV over Wi-Fi. This traffic stays on your local network and is not sent to the developer.

Arc Player does not upload your videos to the developer or to a metadata provider. No user data is sold. The developer does not receive your phone number, videos, watch history, or account identifier.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Videos and media access: to list and play your own media library.
  • Notifications: for optional download progress and completion notices.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: so a download or TV session you started can continue with the screen off. Both use ongoing notifications and stop when finished.
  • Install packages — APK build only: to hand a downloaded update to Android’s installer after you tap Install. This permission is absent from the Google Play build.

Deleting your data

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Arc Player → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Arc Player. This removes the locally stored Telegram session, watch progress, favorites, and preferences.
  2. Delete offline videos from Arc Player’s Downloads page or from Movies/Arc Player/ using a file manager.
  3. Manage or delete your Telegram account, phone number, and cloud videos in Telegram’s own app and account settings.

The developer keeps no server-side copy of your data, so there is nothing additional to request from the developer.

Children

Arc Player is not directed to children. It does not provide a social network, public content feed, or accounts managed by the developer.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

For questions or requests, open an issue in the public Arc Player privacy repository. Do not include phone numbers, login codes, passwords, session files, or private videos in a public issue.
